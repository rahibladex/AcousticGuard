SCENARIO 1

Sudden Ambush / Attack

The Danger: An attacker ambushes from behind or grabs the victim, making it impossible to unlock the screen, open apps, or dial 100.
1
Natural Scream: Edge AI detects distress scream decibels with >80% confidence.
2
Shake SOS: Alternatively, vigorously shaking the phone triggers the 5-sec buffer.
3
Autonomous Defense: Sends live Google Maps GPS pin to all trusted contacts, auto-dials emergency line, and rings sirens.

SCENARIO 2

Late Night Walking Alone

The Danger: Walking through unlit alleys or poorly illuminated streets after work or university.
1
Start Safe Walk: Select a 15 or 30-minute duration before beginning your transit.
2
Continuous Monitoring: Timer runs silently with live GPS fix ready.
3
Auto-Dispatch: If you reach home safely, tap Stop. If immobilized or kidnapped, full SOS fires immediately upon expiry.
🔊
SCENARIO 3

Guardian Asleep in Silent Mode

The Danger: Victim triggers emergency at 2:00 AM, but parents/guardians are fast asleep with phones muted.
1
Keyword Trigger: Incoming SMS contains [TEJASHWINI_SOS_TRIGGER].
2
DND Override: Guardian's TEJASHWINI app wakes screen and forces alarm volume to 100%.
3
Loud Siren & Map: Continuous siren sounds until dismissed; VIEW LIVE LOCATION button opens Google Maps instantly.

SCENARIO 4

Hostile Cab Driver or Stalker

The Danger: A taxi driver locks doors, takes an unfamiliar route, or individuals harass you in public transit.
1
Fake Call Decoy: Tap "Fake Call" to schedule an authentic incoming call in 30s.
2
De-escalation: Answer the ringing phone: "Papa, I'm at the crossing now, police checkpoint is ahead."
3
Silent SOS: Or activate Silent SOS to dispatch GPS tracking without the driver noticing.

SCENARIO 5

Domestic Hostage / Silent Danger

The Danger: Intruders in the house or coercive stalking where sounding a loud siren would incite immediate violence.
1
Enable Silent SOS: Turn on "Silent SOS Mode" in app Settings.

Stealth Trigger: Siren and strobe are muted completely.
3
Silent Beacons: High-precision GPS coordinates are silently dispatched to guardians and authorities.

SCENARIO 6

Stranded with Dying Battery (4%)

The Danger: Stranded in a remote highway or desolate location with phone battery dying rapidly.
1
Auto-Monitoring: Low Battery Alert service constantly observes battery percentage.
2
Threshold Detection: When battery drops below 5%, app triggers automatic safety SMS.

Last Known Pin: Contacts receive: "Low Battery Alert (4%)! Last location: [Google Maps Pin]" before power loss.
