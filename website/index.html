System Architecture

How the Emergency Protocol Executes

From acoustic wave capture to multi-vector beaconing, review the automated lifecycle of an emergency event.

01

Acoustic Sampling

Android Foreground Service samples audio streams via AudioRecord without storing raw audio, converting PCM buffers into decibel amplitudes.

02

Edge AI Inference

TensorFlow Lite analyzes audio spectra against calibrated decibel thresholds (40dB–100dB) to detect distress patterns within milliseconds.

03

5-Second Grace Buffer

Continuous haptic vibration and visible countdown modal give the user 5 seconds to tap "I AM SAFE" and prevent accidental false alarms.

04

Multi-Vector Dispatch

SmsManager sends Google Maps coordinates, AudioRecord captures incident evidence, and ToneGenerator sounds 100% volume alarm siren.

Privacy & Security

Transparent Permission Breakdown

NariShakti SOS is built on a strict zero-telemetry philosophy. Here is why each Android permission is required.

RECORD_AUDIO

Real-Time Audio Analysis

Required to calculate ambient decibels and classify distress acoustics locally. Audio is processed on-device in RAM and never uploaded to any remote server.

ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION

High-Accuracy GPS Fix

Required to obtain fresh latitude and longitude coordinates and generate Google Maps navigation links for SMS distress beacons.

SEND_SMS

Direct Carrier Alerts

Dispatches urgent text messages directly through your mobile network carrier to your trusted circle, functioning even without mobile data/internet.

CAMERA

Strobe Distress Light

Required exclusively for controlling the rear camera LED flash unit as a high-visibility SOS beacon in darkness. No photo or video frames are captured.
